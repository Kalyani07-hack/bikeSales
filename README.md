# Bike Sales Analysis
An interactive Excel dashboard analyzing customer demographics and purchasing behavior to uncover key factors influencing bike purchases.
This project explores a bike sales dataset to understand who buys bikes and why — analyzing patterns across income levels, age groups, commute distances, education, occupation, and region.

Key Insights
- Income & Gender: Male customers with higher average incomes (₹60,124) were more likely to purchase bikes compared to females (₹55,774)
- Age Group: Middle-aged customers had the highest bike purchase rate across all age brackets
- Commute Distance: Customers commuting **0–1 miles** purchased bikes the most, suggesting bikes are preferred for short-distance travel
- Education: Professionals and those with Bachelor's degrees made up the majority of buyers
- Region: North America led in total bike purchases across all regions
  
Tools & Techniques Used
Microsoft Excel - Data cleaning, analysis, dashboard 
PivotTables - Aggregating and summarizing data 
Slicers - Interactive filtering by Region, Gender, Education 
Charts - Bar charts, line charts for visual storytelling
